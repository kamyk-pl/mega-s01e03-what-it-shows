The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so day and month swap: booked 7 April, invite for 4 July. Channels: app and email, and the form on booking-v2. Fix: parse in the tenant’s locale.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field for tenants who write the day first. People type what the hint says, the form reads it the tenant’s way, so the date swaps. The parser fix does not touch this path. Fix: the hint follows the tenant’s locale, as booking-v2’s does.
  • one-day move: no swap. The date moves by one day: the invite says Tuesday, the calendar says Monday; a client in Lisbon and one in Toronto got different days for one meeting. Fix: not known yet; check how invites store and convert times.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line, and none of the subjects names a cause. Intake’s tag guesses from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant, so it does not split them either.
