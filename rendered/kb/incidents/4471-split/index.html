parser: day and month swapped

Ticket: https://github.com/kamyk-pl/mega-s01e03-what-it-shows/issues/1

What people see. A date with day and month swapped: booked 7 April, invite for 4 July. Every date in these reports has a day of 12 or less, because a day above 12 cannot be read as a month.

Who. Every tenant on file writes the day first. The app and email channels on any tenant, and the form on booking-v2 tenants, whose hint says DD/MM.

Fix. The shared parser has to read dates in the tenant’s locale, not month first. This is the fix #4471 already queued.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the month first

Ticket: https://github.com/kamyk-pl/mega-s01e03-what-it-shows/issues/2

What people see. The same swap, but only through the booking form of Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour.

Why it is different. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not touch this path.

Fix. The hint under the field has to follow the tenant’s locale, like booking-v2’s DD/MM/YYYY for day-first tenants.

one-day: the date moves by one day

Ticket: https://github.com/kamyk-pl/mega-s01e03-what-it-shows/issues/4

What people see. Not a swap. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May. A client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting. An invite arrives before the booking was made.

Why it is different. The desk had already read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), but #4471 files them under parser by tenant. They belong to this ticket, not the parser one. The date is converted across time zones, so it lands on the neighbouring day. It needs a separate look at how invites store and send times.

Fix. Unknown yet. Start from these reports.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. These subjects (wrong day, wrong date, dates mixed up) describe a symptom, not a cause, so three causes merged into one ticket.
  • The intake tag is a guess from the browser, and it disagrees with the tenant in both directions.
  • The export repeats some reports across pages. Counting raw rows inflates the queue.

Decision records are never edited after they are accepted, so 0001 needs a successor. Until then, read the body before merging a report about dates.
